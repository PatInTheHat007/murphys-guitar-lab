How it works

Songs first. Theory later.

1. Two chords

You'll learn Em and G and switch between them in time with a band. That's enough to play a real song.

2. Your first notes

Next comes a five-note scale that always sounds good. You'll play it over the band and make up your own little tunes.

3. More when you're ready

Once the basics feel easy, there are more chords and some extra credit on how the neck fits together. No rush.

Practice tips

Four habits that help

Slow is fast

Play slowly enough that you never stop. Speed comes on its own once your hands know the moves.

Count your changes

Set a one-minute timer and count how many clean chord switches you make. Try to beat it tomorrow.

End on a home note

When you make up a tune, finish on a note from the chord the band is playing. It'll sound finished.

Sore fingers are normal

Your fingertips will toughen up in two or three weeks. Short daily sessions help more than long ones.

Questions

Do I need to read music?

No. Everything uses tab and chord pictures, and every lesson explains how to read them.

What guitar do I need?

Any acoustic or electric guitar works. Just make sure it's in tune; a free tuner app on your phone is the easiest way.

How long until I can play a song?

Most people can play Em – G with the band (lesson 5) within a week of 10-minute practice.

Which file do I open?

index.html is this page. murphy-guitar-lab.html has the lessons. Keep both in the same folder.
