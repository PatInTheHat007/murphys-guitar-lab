Practice tips

Four habits that make the difference.

01

Slow is fast

Play slowly enough that you never stop. Speed comes on its own once your hands know the moves.

02

Count your changes

Set a one-minute timer and count your clean chord switches. Try to beat that number tomorrow.

03

End on a home note

When you make up a tune, finish on a note from the band's chord. It'll sound finished.

04

Sore fingers are normal

Fingertips toughen up in two or three weeks. Short daily sessions help more than long ones.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need to read music?

No. Everything uses tab and chord pictures, and the lessons show you how to read them.

What guitar do I need?

Any acoustic or electric guitar works, as long as it's in tune. The first lesson has a tuner that listens through your microphone.

How long until I can play a song?

Most people can play their first song with the backing band within a week of practising 10 minutes a day.

I already play. Where should I start?

Look at the checklist at the end of each level in the full course. Start at the first level where you can't do everything on the list yet.

How do online lessons work?

You join a video call from your computer, tablet or phone. You'll need a quiet room and a camera that shows your fretting hand. After each lesson you get a written practice plan and a recording.

What if I need to cancel a lesson?

Give at least 24 hours' notice and the lesson can be moved to another time for free.
